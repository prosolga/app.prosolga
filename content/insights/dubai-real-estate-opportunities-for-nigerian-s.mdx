Total transactions already exceeding AED 130B+ this year
Strong demand driven by global investors, not just regional buyers

 → Waterfront and ultra-luxury assets
 → Portfolio-based acquisitions tied to residency and wealth structuring

At the mid-level, Nigerian investors are leveraging:
 → Flexible payment plans
 → Low entry thresholds

It’s no longer just a real estate market.
It’s a multi-tier investment ecosystem.
From first-time international investors to seasoned global capital allocators, the market provides:
- Accessibility
- Liquidity
- Scalability
